Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 October 2026

ProofPass is a prototype built for CODE APEX 3.0 (iMocha track, problem IM-03). This policy describes how the full product is designed to handle data. In this prototype, nothing you type or upload is sent anywhere.

What we process

Resume text, public repository metadata and the file contents needed to check a claim, certificate links and details you supply, your target role or job description, and your consent choices.

Why we process it

To extract the skills you claim, check them against evidence, match them to a role, and assemble your passport.

AI processing

Resume text and selected code snippets are sent to a language model that proposes skills. Deterministic code then checks every proposal, so the model never has the final say. You can switch this off per source in the consent settings.

Private repositories

Private repositories are read only when you grant read-only access. We do not keep repository contents after analysis. We keep evidence pointers such as file paths, commit hashes and check results.

Sharing

Your passport is private by default. You choose who receives the link and can revoke it. The public verify page shows only whether a passport hash matches.

Retention and deletion

You can delete your passport and uploaded files at any time. Access tokens are revoked when you delete your account.

Fairness

We do not produce a single hire score. Claims without evidence appear as Claimed and are not penalised. Names can be hidden when recruiters compare candidates.

Security

Access tokens are stored encrypted and all traffic uses HTTPS.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data. Write to privacy@proofpass.example (placeholder address for this prototype).

Changes

We will update this page and its date when the policy changes.
