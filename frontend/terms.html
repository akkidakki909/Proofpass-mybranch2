These terms describe how ProofPass is meant to be used. ProofPass is a hackathon prototype for CODE APEX 3.0 (iMocha track, problem IM-03) and is offered as is.

Your content

Submit only your own work, or work you have permission to use. Do not submit other people's personal information without their consent.

What a passport means

A passport records what ProofPass could verify at the time it was issued. Verified means the checks passed. It is not a guarantee of ability. The hash on a passport shows it has not changed since issue. It does not prove the skills are true.

Not a hiring decision

ProofPass gives evidence, not a single score. People make hiring decisions, and recruiters must not treat a passport as the only basis for one.

Micro-checks

Micro-checks use your own code and commit history. Answers are graded automatically. A failed check never lowers a skill below Supported.

Acceptable use

Do not present someone else's work as yours, try to bypass the checks, or scrape passports you were not given access to.

Availability and liability

The service may change or stop at any time. To the extent the law allows, the ProofPass team is not liable for losses that come from relying on a passport.
